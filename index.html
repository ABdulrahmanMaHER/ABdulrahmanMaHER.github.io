Employee Time Management

Tracks employee weekly hours Through its Policy ... a user can make a custom policy for every employee and the app follow it for example: a policy from Monday to Friday, standard 40 h/week and checks every active employee each Saturday with a scheduled job.

  • Under 40 hours: creates a warning record and emails the employee and all managers.
  • Exactly 40 hours: no action.
  • Over 40 hours: creates an overtime record and emails managers a deep link to it.
  • Managers approve or reject each record. Approved overtime wages accumulate on the employee's total salary and flow into payroll through the Overtime salary rule.
